# Combat implementation and QA — 2026-10-03

The current encounter uses four shared weapon roles and four enemy variants:

| Enemy | Visual identity | Weapon | Behavior |
|---|---|---|---|
| Basic | Red, standard proportions | Pistol | Approaches the player |
| Assault | Orange, compact proportions | Shotgun | Warns, then commits to a charge |
| Sniper | Cyan, tall proportions | Sniper | Warns along a ranged attack lane |
| Heavy | Purple, larger proportions | RPG | Warns before an area attack |

Each acquired weapon supplies exactly one shot. Enemy drops return to the player; a loaded weapon cannot gain an extra shot. An empty player can dodge and bait an Assault into a wall to obtain its Shotgun. Enemy contact kills the player outside the dodge window and restarts the encounter.

Final corrections: stable gameplay shot height and mouse direction, visual weapon meshes without physical collision, persisted Rifle/Sniper Muzzle sockets, pellets ignoring other bullets, enemy capsules participating in sniper hit tests, pickup volumes overlapping only Pawns, and attack warnings removed on death. Pickup volumes no longer block penetration or blast checks.

Main assets changed across this work: `BP_EnemyStraightRunner`, `BP_EnemyRunnerFast`, `BP_EnemyRunnerSlow`, new `BP_EnemySniper`, `BP_ThirdPersonCharacter`, `BP_AmmoHUD`, `BP_Pistol`, `BP_BulletProjectile`, `BP_PistolPickup`, `Gun_Rifle`, `Gun_Sniper`, and `Lvl_ThirdPerson` with its encounter actors. Shared implementations reuse the existing Pistol and enemy parent Blueprints.

Validation: UE 5.8 editor compilation passed for all nine related Blueprints. The final disposable PIE run of `Tools/Test-CombatRoles.py` passed all 111 checks with no errors: weapon acquisition/HUD, one-shot limits, Pistol range and single target, Shotgun spread and short range, Sniper penetration/cover, RPG blast/cover, enemy appearances and matching drops, real charge-wall recovery, empty-state dodge/cooldown, physical enemy contact death, and automatic restart. Runtime QA actors are removed and the background CPU preference is restored. Generated evidence remains under `Saved/` and is not source content.

Manual play path: open `Content/ThirdPerson/Lvl_ThirdPerson`, press Play, use WASD to move, mouse to aim, LMB to fire, Space to dodge, and R to retry. Check the visible warning timing and comfort of the fixed camera. Automated QA calls production gameplay functions; physical mouse/keyboard bindings and subjective game feel still need manual play. This is the current combat slice, not completion of the design brief's five authored stages.

## Enemy commitment and presentation — 2026-10-03

Restored weapon return duration to **0.55 seconds** at the user's request, retaining the empty survival interval between a defeat and rearming. `Fix-CloseDropAcquisition.py` also uses the restored value so rerunning that fix cannot silently restore 0.25 seconds.

Fixed enemies running in place during attack warnings and recovery. The shared parent reuses the existing compatible `A_Dummy_Idle`, `A_Dummy_Walk`, and `A_Dummy_Run` animations: stationary states idle, Heavy cruise walks, and Basic/Assault cruise and charge run at a speed-dependent rate. Cached pose changes preserve animation progression between decision pulses. Chasing robots now face their current movement direction; committed charges retain their locked direction. Death animation resets to normal playback speed and stays protected from pose updates.

Attack warnings now track the player early in their existing windup, then commit for the final 0.4 seconds (Assault/Sniper) or 0.55 seconds (Heavy). The warning shows the actual tracked direction/target, takes the role's orange/cyan/purple tint, and brightens at commitment. Sniper discharge briefly becomes a narrow beam at body height; Heavy discharge flashes its existing ground footprint. Both reuse the existing collision-free TelegraphMesh and disappear after 0.16 seconds. Warning height now uses the target's foot height and a 3-unit lift, derived from the player's actual scaled capsule, fixing smaller Assault warnings sinking below the floor. Damage radius, width, range, total attack duration, cooldown, enemy health, one-shot weapons, and dodge rules are preserved. Shared gameplay remains Blueprint-native and uses the existing decision timer with short cleanup timers, with no new Tick or gameplay asset.

## Forward spawns and combat effects — 2026-10-03

The existing spawn points previously generated enemies at their fixed locations even after the player passed them. `BP_EnemySpawnPoint.UpdateSpawnWindow` now projects the point-to-player offset onto the actual StartPoint→EndPoint course direction. Points open within their editable activation distance and retire permanently for this level run at 250 units ahead or behind. Their existing SpawnNext timer stops on retirement; already spawned pursuers remain. Retreat does not reopen passed points, and a level restart creates fresh points. No new enemy class or spawn manager was added.

Enemy TelegraphMesh now reuses the inspected Engine Plane with the new shared translucent, unlit `Enemies/Materials/M_AttackTelegraph`. Assault/Sniper show a thin outlined directional lane with chevrons; Heavy shows a hollow perimeter. Existing role colors, aim tracking/lock, attack durations, damage and death cleanup are preserved. The old upright opaque cylinder and filled blast disk are gone. The same accurate ground footprint flashes on discharge.

RPG feedback keeps its existing radial damage/cover flow and impact fragments. The old growing brass sphere is replaced with a 70-unit hot core lasting 0.07 seconds, an expanding irregular ground wave, and a bounded 160-unit camera-facing fire/spark flare. Ring/flare use the same shared material and existing 0.02-second visual timer. Flare fades by approximately 0.24 seconds, all visuals hide by 0.35 seconds, and the projectile cleans up at 0.42 seconds. Returning weapons remain at **0.55 seconds**, one shot each, no reload. New visual sizes/durations are Editor tunables.

Existing map actors were tuned: Basic point Y=1700, first delay 1.5s; Assault Y=1300, delay 1.0s; both repeat every 3.2s with their existing two-alive caps. Heavy/Sniper retain Y=2400/3000 with activation distance 1300/1500 and initial delay 1.0s. Runner_EndPoint is now Y=3850, matching this combat slice instead of retaining a ~150m empty tail. Existing completion still means the Complete progress state; no victory transition or checkpoint system was added. Placed Pistol Target, Dummy_Target and Gun_Pistol display actors are hidden and non-colliding during gameplay; their assets remain available for separate tests.

Changed gameplay assets in this pass: the four existing enemy Blueprints, BP_EnemySpawnPoint, BP_BulletProjectile, M_AttackTelegraph, Lvl_ThirdPerson and its existing external actors. The temporary editor queue launcher was removed from the persisted player controller. Editor automation/QA source is under Tools; generated evidence stays under Saved.
